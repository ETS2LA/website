# ETS2LA is Open Source
If you have paid for access, you have been scammed. Please report the website to us on https://ets2la.com/discord and if possible trigger a refund from your payment provider.

Some known scammers:
- **ARC APP** - This is a scam, it is not affiliated with ETS2LA in any way.
- **Installation Support Resellers** - You can find installation tutorials on Youtube. Do not pay people to install ETS2LA.

Possible bad actors:
- **YMN Mods** - We have gotten reports they're trying to clone ETS2LA, **these are unverified and very likely false**. They have however "stolen" (not respected the licenses of) other programs like `tmudge/maps` for their mapping app. I have reached out to clarify, and have not received a response.
